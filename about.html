Product Evolution & Changelog

Continuous development, financial model calibration, and operational milestones of the Financial Guy platform.

v2.2.0 — Open-Source & Cloud Archiving 2026-10

Transitioned from standalone commercial domain hosting to GitHub Pages for permanent, zero-maintenance portfolio accessibility. Standardized documentation and open-sourced core quantitative scripts.

v2.1.0 — Real-Time Market Dashboard & Global Exchange Tracker 2026-06

Integrated TradingView equity/forex heatmaps, a browser-based financial news feed aggregating CNBC, BBC, The Guardian, The New York Times, and The Wall Street Journal through a free RSS conversion API, and an interactive Leaflet.js map tracking real-time trading hours across 14 global stock exchanges (NYSE, TSX, LSE, HKEX, SSE, etc.).

v2.0.0 — Personal Finance & Wealth Management Suite 2026-03

Expanded platform with 5 compact calculators: Mortgage Amortization (Fixed/Variable & Bi-weekly schedules with CSS conic-gradient charts), Investment Compounding (Rule of 72), Inflation-Adjusted Retirement Planner, Progressive Income Tax, and Monthly Budget Analyzer.

v1.0.0 — Initial Launch: Corporate Valuation & DCF Engine 2025-11

Launched the foundational web platform featuring a multi-scenario 5-year FCFF Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) & CAPM WACC valuation model with dual terminal value methodologies.
